# Career Compass

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://career-compass-yhpp.onrender.com/
Career Compass is a web-based career guidance platform designed to help 
12th-grade students navigate one of the most difficult decisions of their 
academic life — choosing the right career path.

## The Problem
Many students completing their 12th grade struggle to decide which stream, 
course, or college best suits their strengths and interests. Without proper 
guidance, this often leads to poorly-informed decisions that affect their 
future academic and career trajectory.

## The Solution
Career Compass conducts a structured assessment for students to evaluate 
their interests, aptitude, and academic performance. Based on the 
assessment results combined with the student's marks, the platform 
generates personalized recommendations for:
- Suitable courses/streams
- Colleges aligned with their profile and location

## Key Features
- Interactive career assessment
- Marks-based, personalized course recommendations
- College recommendations based on student performance and preferences
- District-wise college database (Tamil Nadu)

## Alignment
This project is aligned with **SDG 4 (Quality Education)** and 
**SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth)**, aiming to improve access 
to informed educational and career decision-making for students.

## Tech Stack
- Node.js / Express (backend)
- HTML/CSS/JavaScript (frontend)
- LLM integration (OpenRouter) for intelligent recommendations
